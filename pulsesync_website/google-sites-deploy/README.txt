PULSESYNC WEBSITE - PUBLIC HOSTING QUICK START

The index.html file is a self-contained copy of PulseSync, ready for static web hosting.

1. Open https://app.netlify.com/drop in your browser and sign in or create a Netlify account.
2. Drag the entire google-sites-deploy folder onto the Netlify Drop upload area.
3. Wait for the upload to finish. Netlify will provide a public URL ending in netlify.app.
4. Open that URL in a private/incognito browser window to verify it loads publicly.
5. In Google Sites, open your site in edit mode.
6. Select Insert > Embed > By URL, paste the public Netlify URL, then select Insert.
7. Resize the embedded frame and publish the Google Site.

Custom domain:
After deployment, Netlify's site dashboard can connect a domain you own. Do not use
pulsesync.it.support.co.za until that hostname has working DNS. The site can be
published on the Netlify-provided URL without configuring a custom domain.

Limitations:
- Ticket and team demo data remain in each visitor's browser local storage; they are
  not shared with WordPress, Google Sites, or other visitors.
- The AI Coach is a scripted demonstration, not a live AI service.
- The standalone website is about 3.8 MB; allow the upload to finish.
- No passwords or login codes are needed to embed the published URL in Google Sites.
